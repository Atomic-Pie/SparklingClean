Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions Answered

How long does an oven clean take?

It depends on the complexity, size and state of your appliances. It can be 90 minutes to 6 hours, but we'll let you know the estimated time when we quote.

What areas do you cover?

We are based in Carlow and cover most of County Carlow, County Kilkenny, County Wexford, County Wicklow, County Kildare and County Laois. Send us your location and we'll confirm. We're happy to travel for the right job.

What should I send for a quote?

Just send us photos of what needs cleaning (oven, carpets, sofa, mattress, etc.) and your location. We'll check if we cover your area and provide a quote quickly.

Do I need to do anything before you arrive?

No preparation needed! Just make sure the oven is switched off and cool. We bring all equipment and products needed.

Are the fumes safe? What about pets?

Yes! We use non-caustic, eco-friendly products that are safe for your family and pets. No harsh chemical smell or dangerous fumes.

Do you clean range cookers and AGAs?

Yes! We clean all types of domestic cookers including single ovens, double ovens, range cookers and AGAs across Carlow, Kilkenny, Wexford, Wicklow, Kildare and Laois.

How do you clean carpets?

We use a multi-step preparation process followed by hot/cold water extraction or steam cleaning, depending on the type and condition of your carpet. This gives outstanding results with thorough cleaning deep into the fibres.

Do you need access to water and electricity?

Yes, we'll need access to a power socket and water supply (kitchen tap is perfect). Everything else is provided by us.
